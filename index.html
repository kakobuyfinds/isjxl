Shopping guides

BBDBuy product links and listing details

When you save a seller product link, record the item ID, selected color, size variant and the date you checked it. Two similar listing photos can belong to different products. Open each listing again before placing an order and confirm the current stock status and seller return window.

For a clothing set, check whether the listing includes both pieces and whether each size can be selected separately. For jerseys, verify the name and number on the chosen variant. For underwear, check the pack quantity and the seller’s return terms.

BBDBuy size guide for shoes and clothing

A sneaker size chart may show recommended foot length, insole length or both. Confirm which measurement it uses before comparing EU and US shoe sizes. For hoodies, tees and jackets, lay a garment you already wear flat and compare its chest width, shoulder width, sleeve length and back length with the listing.

Use the waist width, rise and inseam for pants or shorts. Double a flat waist measurement only when the chart specifies circumference. For caps, compare head circumference and the adjustment range.

BBDBuy QC photos and visual checks

Quality-control photos are most informative when they match the selected variant. For sneakers, request a pair photo and look for heel alignment, outsole condition and the size label. For jackets, examine the zipper, seams and any marks in daylight. Compare measurements against a readable ruler when fit is important.

For scarves, ask for the full print and a close-up of the edges. For jewelry, check the clasp, chain length and surface finish. If a listing mentions a specific material, record the seller’s description and request supporting documentation when that detail matters.

BBDBuy shipping checklist

For a landed-cost estimate, separate the item subtotal, domestic shipping, service fees and international freight. Ask which shipping method uses actual parcel weight or volumetric weight, and compare quotes using the same packed dimensions and destination.

Before dispatch, confirm the recipient address, tracking coverage and the process for a delivery claim. Check any applicable import charges for your destination and keep the order confirmation and parcel tracking number together.

Frequently asked questions

What is a BBDBuy spreadsheet?

It is a shopping reference that groups product links and comparison notes by category. This guide uses CAFANS category links and explains how to evaluate listings and product photos.

How do I find the right shoe size?

Measure both feet in centimeters, use the longer measurement, and compare it with the seller’s chart for that model. Check whether the chart gives recommended foot length or the physical insole length.

Are older QC photos useful?

Yes, as a reference for details to inspect. For your decision, look for photos tied to the exact listing, color, and size you selected. Photos alone cannot establish authenticity.

What should I include in an international delivery estimate?

Include the item price, domestic delivery, applicable service fees, international freight, and any destination taxes or carrier charges. Use your destination and the packed parcel dimensions when requesting a shipping quote.
